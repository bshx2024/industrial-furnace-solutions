## 1. The 2026 Summer Reality: Decision 963 & Peak-Hour Shifts

The implementation of Decision 963 has moved the industrial peak-hour window to **5:30 PM – 10:30 PM**. For a reheating furnace, which typically accounts for 60% of a plant's energy load, this shift is critical. 

| Period | Time Window (Decision 963) | Cost Multiplier | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Off-Peak** | 0:00 AM – 6:00 AM | **1.0x** (Base) | High-throughput charging |
| **Normal** | 6:00 AM – 5:30 PM | **2.2x** | Steady-state rolling |
| **Peak** | **5:30 PM – 10:30 PM** | **3.3x** | **AI-Driven Load Shifting** |

Under the **T80 Standard**, our AI systems predict rolling mill requirements 4 hours in advance. By "over-charging" thermal energy into the furnace during normal hours and dropping to a minimum-maintenance state during the 5:30 PM peak window, mills can avoid the highest electricity tariffs without stopping production.

## 2. Technical Module: AI Predictive Stoichiometry for Fuel Flexibility
### The 2026 Challenge: Gas Pressure Fluctuations
As industrial parks prioritize power generation during the heatwave, gas supply pressure to steel mills often fluctuates. Traditional manual controls cannot react fast enough, leading to "rich" combustion (fuel waste) or "lean" combustion (billet oxidation).

### The T80 Solution: Real-Time Stoichiometric Correction
The T80 roadmap integrates real-time flow meters and oxygen analyzers that communicate with our **AI Command Center**. Even if gas pressure drops by 15%, the AI adjusts the air-fuel ratio within 3 seconds to maintain the stoichiometric "sweet spot."

## 3. Technical Module: High-Emissivity "Cold-Wall" Technology
### The Problem: Summer Shell Radiation
When ambient temperatures reach 40°C in Binh Duong, the heat radiating from a traditional furnace shell (often 120°C+) creates an unbearable working environment and wastes massive energy.

### The T80 Solution: Multi-Layer Fiber & Functional Coating
We replace heavy castables with **multi-layer ceramic fiber modules** and apply a **high-emissivity functional coating** to the interior. This "Cold-Wall" approach keeps the furnace shell below 70°C even during the summer peak. This doesn't just save 5% in fuel; it protects your workforce and prevents thermal-induced equipment failure during the high-demand season.
